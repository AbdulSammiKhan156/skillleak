# Job Title: Network Support Officer - Sample Bank, Karachi

## Responsibilities
- Provide first-line network support to 120 branches; troubleshoot branch connectivity outages and restore service quickly.
- Monitor WAN links and network devices through the monitoring system and act on threshold alerts.
- Record every incident in the IT service management tool and write a short incident report for outages longer than 30 minutes.
- Maintain access control lists on branch routers in line with the bank's security policy.
- Coordinate with telecom service providers during link failures.

## Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering or IT.
- Working knowledge of TCP/IP, routing and switching.
- Basic Linux command-line skills.
- Good written and verbal communication.
