# Job Title: NOC Engineer (Fresh Graduate) - Sample ISP, Islamabad

## Role Summary
Our 24/7 Network Operations Centre monitors and maintains the backbone and customer access network. As a NOC Engineer you will be the first responder to network alarms and customer outages.

## Responsibilities
- Monitor network health using NMS dashboards and SNMP-based alarms, and respond to alerts within defined SLAs.
- Troubleshoot connectivity, latency and packet-loss issues across routers and switches, isolating faults layer by layer.
- Log, update and close incident tickets with a clear description of the fault, root cause and corrective action.
- Escalate unresolved faults to Level-2 teams with complete diagnostic evidence.
- Perform basic configuration changes on routers and switches (VLANs, static routes, OSPF) under supervision.

## Requirements
- BE/BS in Electrical, Telecom or Computer Engineering.
- Strong understanding of IP addressing and subnetting.
- Hands-on knowledge of routing and switching.
- Ability to work in rotating shifts and communicate clearly with customers and field teams.
