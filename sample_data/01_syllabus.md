# Course Outline: EE-357 Computer Communication Networks
Department of Electrical Engineering - BE Electrical (Telecom), 6th Semester
Credit Hours: 3 + 1 (Theory + Lab)

## Course Description
This course introduces the architecture, protocols and design principles of computer networks. Students study the layered model of communication, IP addressing, routing and switching, transport protocols, application-layer services and the fundamentals of network security.

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
On successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

## Weekly Lecture Plan
- Week 1: Introduction to networks, topologies, OSI and TCP/IP models
- Week 2: Physical layer, transmission media and Ethernet
- Week 3: Data link layer, MAC addressing, switching and ARP
- Week 4: IPv4 addressing and subnetting
- Week 5: VLSM and IPv6 basics
- Week 6: Static routing and routing tables
- Week 7: Dynamic routing protocols - distance vector and link state
- Week 8: Midterm examination
- Week 9: OSPF single-area operation
- Week 10: VLANs, trunking and inter-VLAN routing
- Week 11: Transport layer - TCP connection management and UDP
- Week 12: Application layer - DNS, DHCP and HTTP
- Week 13: Network troubleshooting methodology - the layered (bottom-up, top-down, divide-and-conquer) approach to fault isolation
- Week 14: Network management fundamentals - introduction to SNMP and network monitoring concepts
- Week 15: Network security basics - firewalls, access control lists and common threats
- Week 16: Wireless LANs (IEEE 802.11) and course review
