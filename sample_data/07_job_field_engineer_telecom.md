# Job Title: Graduate Field Network Engineer - Sample Telecom Vendor, Islamabad

## Responsibilities
- Commission and integrate IP transport equipment at customer sites, configuring interfaces, VLANs and static routes.
- Troubleshoot link and service faults on site using a structured fault-isolation process and confirm restoration with the NOC.
- Use packet captures and device logs to support root-cause analysis of recurring faults.
- Monitor performance counters and alarms during acceptance testing.

## Requirements
- BE Electrical/Telecom.
- Solid understanding of IP networking, subnetting and routing protocols.
- Willingness to travel to sites.
