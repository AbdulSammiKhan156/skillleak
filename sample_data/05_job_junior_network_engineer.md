# Job Title: Junior Network Engineer - Sample Systems Integrator, Lahore

## Responsibilities
- Configure and deploy routers, switches and firewalls for enterprise customers, including VLANs, inter-VLAN routing and OSPF.
- Implement firewall policies and access control lists according to customer security requirements.
- Diagnose and resolve network faults reported by customers, from cabling issues to routing problems.
- Capture and analyse traffic with Wireshark to identify the cause of application slowness.
- Prepare network documentation, including topology diagrams and post-implementation reports.

## Requirements
- Degree in Electrical/Computer Engineering or Computer Science.
- CCNA-level knowledge of routing and switching.
- Good subnetting skills.
- Familiarity with network monitoring tools is a plus.
