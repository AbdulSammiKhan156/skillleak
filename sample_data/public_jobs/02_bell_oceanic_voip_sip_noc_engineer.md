# Job Title: VoIP SIP & NOC Engineer

## Key Responsibilities
- Monitor global VoIP/SIP traffic and system KPIs to maintain optimal performance.
- Troubleshoot SIP-related issues such as one-way audio, failed INVITEs, and registration failures.
- Configure inbound and outbound routes, including DID mapping and SIP trunking.
- Coordinate with carriers to resolve routing and quality issues promptly.
- Maintain logs, prepare NOC reports, and document incidents thoroughly.
- Participate actively in shift-based NOC operations to ensure 24/7 system availability.

## Required Qualifications
- Strong understanding of VoIP protocols including SIP, RTP, SDP, and call flow processes is essential.
- Proficiency in analyzing PCAP and SIP logs using tools like Wireshark, sngrep, and Homer/HEP is necessary.
- Solid Linux command-line skills, particularly with Ubuntu and CentOS distributions, are required.
- Familiarity with NOC operations including traffic monitoring (ASR, ACD, CPS, QoS metrics), alert handling, outage management...
- Effective communication skills for vendor and carrier coordination are essential.
- The ability to prepare detailed reports and maintain accurate documentation is also required.
