# Job Title: Network Engineer Core Operations

Responsible to handle operations, upgradation, and optimization of Wateen IP/MPLS Core network

## Main Responsibilities
- Provide Level 1 & 2 support to customers and internal stakeholders
- Deployment of customers MPLS and internet links, and manage NMS like Cacti, What's UP Gold, Manage Engine App Manager
- Coordination with vendors for operational issues
- 24×7 Availability required to handle network outages and Planned activities

## Required Skills
- 2 – 3 years relevant experience in deployment, configuration and supporting large, complex, TCP/IP/MPLS-based networks.
- Good understanding of TCP/IP, MPLS VPN (L2/L3 VPN), BGP, ISIS, OSPF, Carrier Ethernet, QoS, and IPv6.
- Hands-On with Cisco Routers (7600, ASR900, ASR9000) and switches (6500, Nexus 9000,3750,3400)
- Must have excellent written, oral communication and listening skills
