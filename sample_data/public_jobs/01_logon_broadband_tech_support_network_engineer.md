# Job Title: Technical Support Network Engineer

## Key Responsibilities
- Provide technical support to dealers for internet connectivity and network-related issues.
- Configure and manage network devices such as routers, switches, Optical Line Terminals (OLT), [...] Optical Network Units (ONU), and Optical Network Terminals (ONT).
- Utilize expertise in VLANs, trunks, and Layer 2 tunnels to maintain efficient network segmentation and traffic flow.
- Maintain professional communication with companies and clients via email and Microsoft Teams.
- Support and troubleshoot Fiber to the Home (FTTH), Fiber to the Curb (FTTC), [...] and Gigabit Passive Optical Network (GPON) technologies.
- Address and resolve network system complaints promptly to ensure customer satisfaction.
- Communicate effectively with users to provide network support and resolve issues efficiently.

## Required Qualifications
- 1 to 2 years of experience in the telecommunications industry.
- Strong knowledge of VLANs, trunks, Layer 2 tunnels, and network device configuration.
- Familiarity with FTTH, FTTC, GPON, and managed services.
- Proven ability to troubleshoot and resolve network issues efficiently.

## Preferred
- Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) certification is preferred.
